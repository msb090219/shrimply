## Recommended sequence

1. **U08 Progress:** finish the existing history surface. Show useful monitored-time
   and coverage context, distinguish no data from poor posture, and expose chart
   values without relying on color. Do not invent a clinical health score.
2. **U06 Appearance:** let users preview the existing mascot options and ensure
   the real overlay fits. Keep the separate desktop shrimp distinct from the metric.
3. **U03 Advanced settings:** keep common controls easy to find and move technical
   timing controls out of their way.
4. **One consolidated desktop acceptance pass** for the accepted UI: Main/Settings/
   tray, latest metric dragging, paused preview, calibration interruptions and
   completion, break actions, keyboard navigation, and DPI. Avoid treating every
   historical layout revision as a separate unfinished feature.
5. **Release gates:** address T08, complete the remaining H01–H06 scenarios,
   validate Windows distribution P01, and update/verify W01. macOS packaging is
   intentionally paused in CI and needs its own platform work later.

Calibration's intended tall/normal/hunch roles and implemented safeguards are
documented; neither the earlier successful session nor the UI cleanup establishes
accuracy across users, lighting, occlusion, and camera placement. Preserve that
distinction when closing U02/H06/T08.
