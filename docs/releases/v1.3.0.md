# Shrimply v1.3.0

Unshrimp is now **Shrimply** — don't get shrimped.

## What's new on Windows

- A clearer Dashboard with monitored time, weekly posture summaries and a
  26-week activity calendar.
- Dashboard and Settings in one window, with a collapsible sidebar.
- Horizontal or vertical floating posture meters, with size, placement,
  transparency and motion controls.
- A shrimp or discreet text cue that responds to sustained poor posture,
  with adjustable appearance and recovery delays.
- Sitting-based break reminders with configurable intervals and break lengths.
- Clearer calibration guidance, camera recovery, Light/Dark/device themes,
  and more consistent previews and controls.
- Safer startup settings, calibration saves and history persistence.

## Windows download

Download `shrimply-win64.zip`, extract the entire ZIP, then open
`shrimply/shrimply.exe`. Python is not required; the posture models are included.
The portable app is unsigned, so Windows may show a SmartScreen warning.

## Upgrading from Unshrimp

Quit Unshrimp from its tray menu before opening Shrimply. Existing settings,
calibration and history are copied to `%APPDATA%\Shrimply`; the old files are
kept. An enabled Windows login entry is updated to the new executable location.
Keep the extracted app in a permanent location if you enable startup at login.

Closing the main window leaves Shrimply in the system tray. Choose **Quit**
from the tray menu to exit completely.

## Privacy and limitations

Camera processing stays on your device; camera frames are not saved. The bundled
MediaPipe Tasks SDK sends performance and usage metrics to Google. Shrimply is a
habit reminder, not a medical device. Lighting, framing and occlusion can affect
posture detection; recalibrate after moving your camera or chair.

Mac download and platform-specific notes will be added after the Mac package
has been verified for the joint release.
